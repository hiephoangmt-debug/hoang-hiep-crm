US travel magazine Travel + Leisure has just published its list of “The 50 Best Places to Travel in 2027”, and Da Nang is the only Vietnamese destination to make the cut. The city sits in the Cultural Immersion category, alongside Brazil, Croatia, Malta and Luxor (Egypt).

Why Da Nang made the list

According to Travel + Leisure, what sets Da Nang apart is not just its beaches. From the city, it takes less than an hour to reach two UNESCO World Heritage Sites, Hoi An Ancient Town and the My Son Sanctuary, and you can carry on to Hue over the Hai Van Pass. Having the sea, a modern city, Cham heritage and a former imperial capital all within one region is an advantage very few destinations can match.

The 2027 list was chosen by the magazine’s editors with input from its A-List network of travel advisors. Beyond a destination’s existing appeal, the editors also looked at what is new: hotels, resorts, events and experiences opening in the coming year.

The numbers worth knowing

  • Interest in Vietnam, and in Da Nang in particular, has risen by more than 200%, according to figures shared at the Travel + Leisure World’s Best Summit (July 2026).
  • Skyscanner searches for Da Nang have grown by more than 125% over the past 12 months.
  • Priceline has also named Da Nang one of its standout destinations for 2027.

Tips for travellers

When a destination lands on international lists like this, visitor numbers usually climb noticeably the following year, especially travellers from Europe and North America and during peak seasons. A few suggestions from the DanangStay team:

  • Book early for summer 2027 and public holidays. My Khe, Man Thai – Son Tra and the beachfront resorts at Non Nuoc tend to sell out first in June–August and during the fireworks season.
  • Make time for culture. Rather than just a beach break, set aside one day for Hoi An – My Son and one day for Hue. Four to five days is about right.
  • Try everyday local life. Have mi Quang for breakfast, drink coffee on a pavement stool, browse Han Market, and wander Thanh Ha pottery village or Tra Que vegetable village, just as locals do.
  • Avoid peak hours at the big sights. Ba Na Hills, the Golden Bridge and Hoi An Ancient Town are busiest from 9 to 11 am; going early is cooler and better for photos.

A 4-day “cultural immersion” itinerary

  1. Day 1 – Da Nang: Museum of Cham Sculpture, Linh Ung Pagoda on Son Tra, then an evening stroll by the Dragon Bridge and the Han River.
  2. Day 2 – My Son and Hoi An: My Son Sanctuary early in the morning, the Ancient Town in the late afternoon and evening, and floating a paper lantern on the Hoai River.
  3. Day 3 – Craft villages: Thanh Ha pottery village, the Bay Mau coconut forest and a cooking class.
  4. Day 4 – Hue: over the Hai Van Pass to the Imperial City, Tu Duc Tomb and an evening of ca Hue folk music on the Perfume River.
