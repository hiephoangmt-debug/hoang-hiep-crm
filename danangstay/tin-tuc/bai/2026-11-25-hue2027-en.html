Hue is lining up a packed 2027 calendar for culture-loving travellers: the Hue International Festival Week, the International Light Festival on the Perfume River (Song Huong), and a heritage music week at the end of summer. The plans were announced on 23 November at a conference held by the Hue City People's Committee on its schemes to develop the cultural industries and host national and international events in 2026–2030. Hue is just over the Hai Van Pass from Da Nang, so these dates are well worth building into a trip to central Vietnam.

Hue bets on its cultural industries

At the conference, Vice Chairwoman of the City People's Committee Tran Huu Thuy Giang stressed that Hue is a place where royal heritage, folk culture, craft villages, cuisine and the arts all come together, and that the city sees culture as its spiritual foundation, its inner strength and a new driver of growth. The chosen direction is to link heritage conservation with creativity and innovation, new technology and investment.

The plan focuses on 6 key cultural industry groups:

  1. Cultural tourism.
  2. Performing arts.
  3. Creative design: ao dai, cuisine and film.
  4. Digital cultural products.
  5. Handicrafts and craft villages.
  6. Television and radio, publishing and advertising.

International events planned for 2027

Hue's Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism presented a list of programmes and events planned for 2027–2030. For travellers, the 2027 dates worth noting are:

  • Hue International Festival Week: 6–10 June 2027. Street art, international traditional performances, heritage fine-art exhibitions, antiques showcases and food.
  • Hue International Light Festival 2027: 11–13 June. Held around the park and Memorial area on the south bank of the Perfume River, with international light-art shows, a drone show themed on Hue's heritage and hands-on activities.
  • Hue Wonderverse Music Fest – Hue International Music and Heritage Week: 28 August – 1 September 2027. A mix of Vietnamese music, electronic music and international artistic exchange.

Please note these dates are provisional; the detailed schedule may change once the organisers make their official announcement.

"Nhà sáng tạo Huế – The Hue Creators"

Another new idea is "Nhà sáng tạo Huế – The Hue Creators", a multinational creative reality TV show that combines experiences of Hue's heritage and culture with content creation. The first season is planned for 2027, piloted in Southeast Asia and extended to South Korea; in 2028 the city will study wider cooperation across Asia, and from 2029 it will gradually aim for the global market. The goal is to build a community of creators and bring Hue's culture and heritage closer to audiences and markets.

Tips for travellers

  • Combine the two June weeks. The Festival (6–10 June) runs straight into the Light Festival (11–13 June), so a trip of about a week lets you catch both. Book your Hue hotel early, as rooms usually sell out during festival time.
  • Stay in Da Nang and visit Hue as a day trip, or stay one night. Driving through the Hai Van Tunnel is fastest; for the views, take the Hai Van Pass road and stop at Lang Co. On light-show nights, stay over in Hue so you are not travelling back late.
  • Be ready for the heat. June and late August are hot and sunny in Hue. Visit the Imperial City and the royal tombs early in the morning, then head to the riverbank in the evening for the events. Bring a hat, water and sunscreen.
  • Eat your way through Hue: bun bo Hue, com hen (clam rice), banh beo, banh nam, banh loc and Hue-style che (sweet soups) are easy to find around the city centre.
  • Check the official schedule on the City of Hue's channels before you go, as exact times and venues may change.
