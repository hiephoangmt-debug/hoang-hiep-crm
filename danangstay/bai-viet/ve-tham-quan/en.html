Da Nang

01Hoa Vang · ~40 km from the centre

Sun World Ba Na Hills

A cable car over the forest to a summit of almost 1,500 m: the Golden Bridge held by giant stone hands, the French Village, Le Jardin d'Amour gardens, Debay wine cellar and the indoor Fantasy Park. From 2026 the cable-car ticket can be used for up to 3 consecutive days.

  • ⏱ Time neededFull day (7–8 hours)
  • 🌤 Best timeEarly morning on a clear day, avoid weekends and holidays
  • 👥 Good forAll ages, families, couples
  • 🚗 Getting there~50 min by car
Tips & good to know
  • Take the cable car at 7–8 am to photograph the Golden Bridge before clouds and tour groups arrive.
  • The summit is 6–8°C cooler than the city and can drop below 15°C in winter — bring a jacket.
  • Tickets with a lunch buffet are easier than buying food on the mountain; bring water and sunscreen.
  • In thick fog you will barely see the views — better to change the date; we advise based on the forecast.

02Hoa Vang · ~30 km from the centre

Than Tai Hot Springs Park

A hot-spring park in the forest below Than Tai mountain: mineral pools, mud baths, a Japanese-style onsen, a water park, a wave pool and plenty for kids.

  • ⏱ Time neededHalf to full day
  • 🌤 Best timeCool or drizzly days (November–February)
  • 👥 Good forFamilies, older travellers, groups
  • 🚗 Getting there~40 min by car
Tips & good to know
  • Bring swimwear, a towel, a change of clothes and a bag for wet things.
  • Soak in hot mineral water for 15–20 minutes at a time, drink water and rest between soaks.
  • Anyone with heart conditions or high blood pressure, and pregnant women, should check with a doctor first.
  • Easy to pair with Ba Na Hills on the same road over two days.

03Lien Chieu · foot of the Hai Van Pass

Suoi Luong Eco-tourism Area

A clear, rocky stream running down the mountain towards Nam O beach, with forest huts, stream bathing, picnics and rustic food such as free-range chicken and stream fish — a weekend favourite of Da Nang locals.

  • ⏱ Time neededHalf day
  • 🌤 Best timeDry season April–August, mornings
  • 👥 Good forGroups of friends, families, picnics
  • 🚗 Getting there~30 min by car
Tips & good to know
  • In the rainy season (October–December) the stream can rise quickly and the site may close temporarily — always check opening first.
  • Never go into the stream after heavy rain or when the water is murky and fast.
  • Wear strapped sandals — the rocks are very slippery; children need an adult with them.
  • Combine with sunset on the Hai Van Pass or the Nam O fishing village.
Check Suoi Luong opening
04Hoa Vang · ~25 km from the centre

Hoa Phu Thanh Adventure Park

White-water rafting down a long rocky stream, forest ziplines, stream swimming and plenty of outdoor games.

  • ⏱ Time neededHalf day
  • 🌤 Best timeDry season, moderate water levels
  • 👥 Good forYoung groups, team building
  • 🚗 Getting there~40 min by car
Tips & good to know
  • Rafting depends on water levels and may pause in heavy rain for safety.
  • Wear quick-dry clothes, strapped sandals and glasses with a strap; bring a waterproof phone pouch.
  • Always wear the life jacket and helmet and listen to the briefing before boarding.
  • Not suitable for very young children or people with heart conditions — ask about age limits first.

07Ngu Hanh Son · ~8 km from the centre

Marble Mountains

Five limestone peaks with caves such as Huyen Khong and Am Phu, Tam Thai and Linh Ung pagodas, and the Non Nuoc stone-carving village below. A lift goes up Thuy Son mountain.

  • ⏱ Time needed2–3 hours
  • 🌤 Best timeEarly morning, fine in light rain
  • 👥 Good forAll ages (with the lift)
  • 🚗 Getting there~15 min by car
Tips & good to know
  • Go at opening time for cool air and fewer people; caves get stuffy at midday.
  • Wear shoes with good grip — old stone steps are slippery when damp.
  • Dress modestly in pagodas and keep quiet in the caves.
  • When buying stone carvings in Non Nuoc, compare prices and confirm shipping costs.

10Son Tra · ~10 km from the centre

Son Tra Peninsula Tour

A guided drive to Linh Ung pagoda, Ban Co peak, the thousand-year-old banyan tree and bay viewpoints; with luck you may spot the rare red-shanked douc langur.

  • ⏱ Time neededHalf day
  • 🌤 Best timeEarly morning after rain
  • 👥 Good forNature lovers, photographers
  • 🚗 Getting there~20 min by car
Tips & good to know
  • The road is steep and winding — do not ride a motorbike yourself on rainy days.
  • Never feed the langurs; keep quiet and keep your distance.
  • Bring water and a hat; there are few shops on the peninsula.
  • Finish with a seafood meal at the Man Thai fishing village.

06Duy Xuyen · ~1 hour from Hoi An

My Son Sanctuary

A valley of Cham temple towers more than a thousand years old, listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site; the ticket includes the electric shuttle and Cham dance performances on schedule.

  • ⏱ Time neededHalf day
  • 🌤 Best timeEarly morning; 'Echoes of My Son' on the 15th of each month
  • 👥 Good forHistory and architecture lovers
  • 🚗 Getting there~1 hour by car from Hoi An
Tips & good to know
  • Go early to avoid harsh sun and afternoon rain, and for beautiful light on the brick walls.
  • Bring water, a hat and walking shoes — the site is large with little shade.
  • Visit the Museum of Cham Sculpture first to understand the story.
  • Try My Son in the morning and Hoi An Ancient Town in the afternoon.

Hue

Hue Imperial City & Royal Tombs

The Imperial City and the tombs of emperors Tu Duc, Khai Dinh and Minh Mang — part of the UNESCO-listed Complex of Hue Monuments. Single and combined tickets are available.

  • ⏱ Time needed1–2 days
  • 🌤 Best timeMornings; spring and summer are drier
  • 👥 Good forHistory and culture lovers
  • 🚗 Getting thereTrain or car, 2.5–3 hours
Tips & good to know
  • A combined ticket saves money if you visit two sites or more.
  • The tombs are far apart and far from the citadel — hire a car for the day.
  • Bring an umbrella and shoes with grip: Hue is rainy and the brick courtyards get slippery.
  • The Imperial City sometimes opens at night — ask us for dates.

General tips for Da Nang, Hoi An and Hue

  • Check the weather the day before. Ba Na, the Cham Islands, Suoi Luong and Hoa Phu Thanh depend heavily on it; in the rainy season (October–December) always have a plan B.
  • Go early. Most sights are busiest from 9 to 11 am; arriving at opening time is cooler, quieter and better for photos.
  • Dress for it. A jacket for Ba Na and Hue in winter, swimwear for hot springs and water parks, modest clothing for pagodas, temples and tombs.
  • E-tickets. Save the QR code on your phone and take a screenshot in case there is no signal in the mountains.
  • Children. Many places price by height — tell us your child's height when booking.
  • Water safety. Stay out of streams after heavy rain and always wear a life jacket on boats.
  • Changes & cancellations. Issued tickets are often non-refundable; check the terms before paying.
  • Avoid unofficial tickets. Buy only from sellers who confirm your booking clearly; avoid resold or suspiciously cheap tickets.
