Our Hue travel guide in one paragraph: go in the dry season (roughly February to August), give one full day to the Imperial City and two royal tombs, take a dragon boat up the Perfume River to Thien Mu Pagoda in the late afternoon, listen to ca Huế (Hue's traditional chamber music) on the river at night, and don't leave without trying bún bò Huế, cơm hến and the little steamed cakes bánh bèo, bánh nậm and bánh lọc. Hue is about 100 km from Da Nang: the train over the Hai Van coast, a car through the Hai Van tunnel or over the pass all take roughly 2 to 4 hours. In 2026 an adult Imperial City ticket costs about 200,000 VND, and the Imperial City + three tombs combo about 530,000 VND (indicative prices).

Hue was the capital of the 13 Nguyễn emperors (1802–1945). The Complex of Hue Monuments became Vietnam's first UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1993, and since 1 January 2025 Hue has been a centrally governed city. This guide was written by Da Nang locals for travellers doing a Da Nang to Hue day trip or staying a night or two.

Things to do in Hue: 10 experiences

1. The Imperial City

Enter through the Ngọ Môn (Meridian Gate), cross the Trung Đạo bridge over the lotus ponds to the Thái Hòa Palace where emperors held court, then continue to the Forbidden Purple City, the Thế Miếu temple and the Nine Dynastic Urns. This is the heart of any Hue trip.

  • Time needed: 2–3 hours.
  • Best time: at opening, or after 15:00.
  • Tip: hire a guide or audio guide; the grounds are huge, so wear walking shoes.

2. Thien Mu Pagoda

Founded in 1601 on a hill above the north bank of the Perfume River, its seven-storey Phước Duyên tower is the symbol of Hue.

  • Time needed: 45–60 minutes.
  • Best time: early morning or late afternoon.
  • Tip: arrive by dragon boat from the city centre to combine it with the river.

3. Tu Duc Tomb

The most romantic of the tombs, with Lưu Khiêm lake, the Xung Khiêm pavilion and pine woods — Emperor Tự Đức used it as a retreat to rest and write poetry while still alive.

  • Time needed: about 1 hour.
  • Best time: morning, when the light on the lake is soft.
  • Tip: pair it with Khai Dinh, which is fairly close.

4. Khai Dinh Tomb

Built of concrete in a blend of Eastern and Western styles; the highlight is the Thiên Định palace covered in mosaics of porcelain and glass. Expect steep stairs.

  • Time needed: 45–60 minutes.
  • Best time: morning or late afternoon; the steps get very hot at midday.
  • Tip: older travellers can take it slowly and rest on the terraces.

5. Minh Mang Tomb

Majestic and perfectly symmetrical along one axis, surrounded by lakes and pine hills — the pick for lovers of classical architecture.

  • Time needed: about 1 hour.
  • Best time: afternoon.
  • Tip: it is the farthest from the centre (about 12 km), so a private car makes sense.

6. Dragon boat on the Perfume River

Dragon boats cruise the Perfume River and stop at Thien Mu Pagoda. Rent a whole boat for your group or join a tour.

  • Time needed: 1–2 hours.
  • Best time: late afternoon, for sunset on the water.
  • Tip: agree the price, stops and return time before boarding.

7. Ca Huế music on the river

Ca Huế is Hue's musical heritage: in the evening you listen to the singers on a boat mid-river and float a paper lantern at the end. Shared-boat tickets are about 100,000 VND for Vietnamese and 150,000 VND for foreign guests (indicative).

  • Time needed: about 1 hour.
  • Best time: sessions usually around 19:00 and 20:00.
  • Tip: book ahead, sit near the bow to hear well, and silence your phone.

8. Truong Tien Bridge and the riverside walk

The six-span steel bridge across the Perfume River changes colour at night. Both banks have walkways, parks and cafés.

  • Time needed: 1 hour.
  • Best time: after 18:30 when the lights come on.
  • Tip: finish an evening of ca Huế with a bowl of chè (sweet soup).

9. Dong Ba Market

Hue's biggest and oldest market — for sesame candy (mè xửng), sour shrimp (tôm chua), conical hats and cheap local dishes.

  • Time needed: 45–60 minutes.
  • Best time: morning.
  • Tip: ask the price first, and mind your wallet in the crowds.

10. Vong Canh Hill, Tam Giang Lagoon and Lang Co

Vọng Cảnh Hill overlooks a bend of the Perfume River and is loveliest at sunset. With more time, watch the sunset over Tam Giang Lagoon, or stop at Lang Co bay on the way back to Da Nang.

  • Time needed: 1–3 hours depending on the stop.
  • Best time: late afternoon.
  • Tip: Tam Giang is a fair drive from the centre — go by private car and head back before dark.

Hue itineraries: 1 day and 2 days

Day trip from Da Nang

  1. 6:30 — Leave Da Nang, cross the Hai Van Pass, stop at the Hai Van Gate and Lang Co.
  2. 9:30–12:00 — Imperial City.
  3. 12:00–13:00 — Lunch: bún bò Huế or bánh bèo, nậm and lọc.
  4. 13:30–15:30 — Tu Duc and Khai Dinh tombs.
  5. 16:00–16:45 — Thien Mu Pagoda.
  6. 17:00 — Back through the Hai Van tunnel, arriving around 19:00–19:30.

Hue in 2 days, 1 night

  1. Day 1 morning: Imperial City, lunch in the centre.
  2. Day 1 afternoon: dragon boat to Thien Mu Pagoda, sunset on the river.
  3. Day 1 evening: ca Huế, Truong Tien Bridge, chè.
  4. Day 2 morning: cơm hến for breakfast, then Minh Mang, Khai Dinh and Tu Duc tombs.
  5. Day 2 afternoon: Dong Ba Market for souvenirs, Vong Canh Hill, then train or car back to Da Nang.

Local tips and dress code

  • Dress: at pagodas, the Thế Miếu and other shrines, cover shoulders and knees, and take off your hat inside halls.
  • Don't sit on or climb artefacts, old balustrades or the dynastic urns, and don't touch lacquered and gilded surfaces.
  • Umbrella and grippy shoes: Hue is rainy and the brick courtyards get slippery.
  • Summer: bring water, a hat and sunscreen — the big courtyards have little shade.
  • Áo dài photos: many visitors rent an áo dài or royal-style costume for photos in the Imperial City; early morning gives the best light.
  • Getting to the tombs: they are spread out, so a car for the day is easier than ride-hailing each leg.

FAQ

How long does it take from Da Nang to Hue?

About 2–2.5 hours by car through the Hai Van tunnel, plus about an hour if you take the pass with photo stops. The train takes about 2.5–4 hours depending on the service.

Is one day enough for Hue?

Enough for the highlights: the Imperial City, two tombs and Thien Mu Pagoda. Stay a night if you want the dragon boat, ca Huế and plenty of food.

How much is a Hue Imperial City ticket?

About 200,000 VND for adults and 40,000 VND for children aged 7–12 (indicative, 2026). For two or more sites, a combo ticket works out cheaper.

Which tomb should I choose if I'm short on time?

Tu Duc and Khai Dinh: close together and completely different — one poetic, one ornate.

Does the Imperial City open in the evening?

In certain seasons and during events, not every night. Ask Vân Trần for the week you travel.

Should I visit Hue in the rainy season?

October to December brings heavy rain and possible floods. If you go, pick days with a better forecast, keep plans flexible and focus on the Imperial City, ca Huế and food.
