Ba Na Hills began as a French hill station in the 1920s, a cool refuge from the summer heat. Today it is a mountaintop resort reached by one of the world's longest cable cars, with gardens, a mock-French village, a pagoda and, since 2018, the Golden Bridge — a 150-metre walkway that seems to be held in the air by two giant moss-covered hands.

The secret is timing

By late morning, tour buses arrive and clouds often roll over the summit. The visitors who love Ba Na are the ones who arrive when the cable car opens on a weekday, walk the Golden Bridge in the soft early light, and head down after lunch.

A suggested route

  1. Cable car up to the Golden Bridge station — the bridge comes first, before the crowds.
  2. Le Jardin d'Amour flower gardens and Linh Ung Pagoda on the mountain.
  3. The French Village: cobbled squares, a cathedral replica and cafés.
  4. Fantasy Park, an indoor amusement park — good for children on a rainy day.
  5. Lunch at the summit, then the cable car down by early afternoon.

Tickets and practicalities

  • The entrance ticket includes the cable cars; lunch-buffet combos are available. Buy online or at the gate — prices change seasonally.
  • The summit is often 6–8 °C cooler than the coast and can be misty or wet. A light jacket and an umbrella are wise.
  • Wear comfortable shoes: there is a lot of walking and some slopes.
  • Go by Grab, private car or hotel shuttle; the base station has large car parks.
Local tip

Check the weather the night before. If the forecast shows heavy cloud on the mountain, swap Ba Na with a beach or Hoi An day — the view is the whole point.

Is it worth it?

Ba Na is unashamedly a theme park, and purists may find it artificial. But the cable car ride over the rainforest is spectacular, and standing on the Golden Bridge above the clouds is one of the most memorable moments of any trip to Vietnam. Go early, and you will understand why.
