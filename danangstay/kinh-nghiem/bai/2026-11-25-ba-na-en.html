The short answer: pick a clear weekday, be at the cable car station when it opens (around 8 am), go straight to the Golden Bridge before anything else, then explore the French Village, the gardens and Fantasy Park, and head down by mid-afternoon — or stay on for Ba Na by Night. In 2026 an adult cable car ticket costs a little under one million VND (indicative), and from 2026 a ticket can be upgraded to cover up to three consecutive days.

Ba Na Hills began as a French hill station in the 1920s, a cool refuge from the summer heat. Today Sun World Ba Na Hills sits on Chua Mountain at almost 1,500 m, reached by cable cars over the rainforest, with a mock-French village, flower gardens, an old wine cellar, a mountain pagoda and, since 2018, the Golden Bridge — a walkway of about 150 m that seems to be held in the air by two giant moss-covered stone hands. How the 3-day ticket works

Since 1 January 2026, Sun World Ba Na Hills has run a “one ticket, three days” policy. According to the park's notice, the benefit applies to visitors who upgrade a one-day ticket to three days at the ticket counter and register FaceID before first use. The three days must be consecutive from activation. It is most useful in the misty season: if day one is clouded in, come back the next morning.

Time-limited deals (an under-25 ticket, discounts through partner apps) appear during the year. To book the right ticket for the right date, see our Da Nang attraction tickets page or message Vân Trần for a current quote.

Best time to visit: hour and month

Time of day: board the first cable car around 8 am (indicative — operating hours can change by season). Tour groups arrive from about 9:30, and by late morning clouds often roll over the summit. On an evening combo, go up in the afternoon.

What to see, zone by zone

The cable cars

Lines include Suoi Mo – Ba Na, Hoi An – Marseille, Debay – Morin, Toc Tien Waterfall – L'Indochine and Bordeaux – Louvre. The ride over primary forest and waterfalls is a highlight in itself; which lines run varies by day, so check the board at the station.

Golden Bridge Da Nang (Cầu Vàng)

About 150 m long, opened in 2018 and famous for its giant stone hands. It is the busiest spot on the mountain — be there before 9:30. Shoot from either end of the bridge to get the hands in frame.

Le Jardin d'Amour

A series of French-style themed gardens, prettiest in the morning.

Debay Wine Cellar

A French-era cellar built into the mountain, cool all year, with a wine bar and atmospheric corridors.

Linh Ung Pagoda Ba Na

A mountain pagoda with a large Buddha statue overlooking the valley. Dress modestly.

French Village

Cobbled squares, a church, hotels and cafés in French style. Afternoon parades and street performances; beautiful when lit up at night.

Fantasy Park

An indoor amusement park with thrill rides and games for small children — a lifesaver on rainy or cold days.

Ba Na by Night

Go up in the afternoon for sunset, dinner, shows and the illuminated French Village. Evening cable cars run on fixed slots, and nights at the top are cold.

Food on the mountain

  • Buffets: mainly at lunchtime (typically from about 10:30 to early afternoon), Asian, Western and Vietnamese dishes.
  • Food vouchers: usable at restaurants and stalls — good for light eaters.
  • Cafés and snacks: plentiful in the French Village, but pricier than in town.

What to wear and bring

  • A light jacket in summer, a warm layer from November to February.
  • Comfortable trainers — lots of walking, slopes and steps.
  • Compact raincoat or umbrella, sunscreen, sunglasses.
  • Power bank and water.
  • ID or passport if you need FaceID registration or a discounted ticket.
  • Covered shoulders and knees for the pagoda.

With children and older travellers

  • Children are priced by height: usually free under 1 m, child fare from 1 to 1.4 m, adult fare above 1.4 m (indicative — confirm when buying).
  • Bring a foldable stroller; the French Village has many steps.
  • Older travellers should choose a sunny day, avoid the cold months and rest between cable car lines.
  • Anyone with heart conditions, high blood pressure or a fear of heights should check with a doctor — some lines run very high.

Combine with Than Tai hot springs

Than Tai Mountain hot springs (Núi Thần Tài) lie on the same road, about 30 km from the centre. A good two-day plan: Ba Na early on day one, then mineral pools and mud baths the next day to rest your legs. A full-day private car is the easiest way to do both.

Local tips

  • Check the mountain forecast the night before. If heavy cloud is expected, swap Ba Na for a beach or Hoi An day — the view is the whole point. We can advise on switching dates.
  • Buy tickets in advance; allow extra time if you want the 3-day upgrade and FaceID.
  • Golden Bridge first, French Village later — move against the tour groups.
  • The queue down can be long in late afternoon; go down before 4 pm or after the rush.

Is it worth it?

Ba Na is unashamedly a theme park, and purists may find it artificial. But the cable car ride over the rainforest is spectacular, and standing on the Golden Bridge above the clouds is one of the most memorable moments of any trip to central Vietnam. Go early, and you will understand why.

FAQ

How much is the Ba Na Hills ticket in 2026?

Roughly 900,000 – 1,000,000 VND for an adult cable car ticket, 700,000 – 800,000 VND for children 1 – 1.4 m, and 1,200,000 – 1,300,000 VND with a lunch buffet (indicative). Prices change by season — check before you go.

Is the 3-day cable car ticket real?

Yes. From 1 January 2026 a ticket can be upgraded from one day to three consecutive days at the ticket counter, with FaceID registration before first use.

What time should I go to Ba Na Hills?

Be at the station around 7:45 – 8:15 for the first cable car and reach the Golden Bridge before 9:30, before the groups and the midday cloud.

Is Ba Na by Night worth it?

Yes if you enjoy the lights, evening shows and don't want an early start. The downside: it is colder and the Golden Bridge photos are not as good as in the morning.

How far is Ba Na Hills from Da Nang?

About 40 km, 45 – 60 minutes by car. Plan 6 – 8 hours on the mountain to see the main zones.

Is December a good month for Ba Na Hills?

It is possible but cold (sometimes below 15°C) and often misty. Bring warm clothes, watch the forecast and consider the 3-day ticket so you can return on a clear day.
