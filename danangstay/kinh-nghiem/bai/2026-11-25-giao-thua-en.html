New Year's Eve in Da Nang 2027 centres on the Bach Dang pedestrian street and the Han River. The countdown show "Da Nang – Touching the Moment of 0h" starts at 23:50 on 31 December 2026. On the stroke of midnight, decorated boats on the river fire fireworks, the Dragon Bridge breathes fire and sprays water, and ships in the port sound their horns to welcome the new year. For a good view, be at the riverside before 22:00 and book a room near the Han River as early as you can.

This guide was put together by locals from the New Year Festival 2027 plan as reported in the Vietnamese press. It covers the schedule of each event, the best spots to watch the fireworks, how to get around on the night of 31 December, and quieter countdown spots for families.

Where are the Da Nang 2027 New Year fireworks, and how long do they last?

This year Da Nang is not staging a high-altitude fireworks show like the DIFF festival. Instead there are two kinds of non-explosive military-grade fireworks, fired low and close to the crowd:

  • From the decorated boats: at 23:55 the boats gather between the Dragon Bridge and Tran Thi Ly Bridge. At exactly midnight they fire fireworks for about 3–5 minutes.
  • Along Bach Dang pedestrian street: around 300 fireworks placed along a stretch of roughly 1.5 km, firing for about 3–5 minutes at the same time as the Dragon Bridge breathes fire.

The fireworks last only a few minutes, so where you stand matters more than when you arrive: pick the stretch of river between the Dragon Bridge and Tran Thi Ly Bridge, where the boats gather.

Best places to watch the countdown and fireworks

1. Bach Dang pedestrian street, half-moon plaza (west bank)

This is the heart of the night: the main stage, the LED countdown screen and fireworks right beside you. The trade-off is very big crowds. Arrive before 21:00 if you want to stand near the stage.

2. West bank between Dragon Bridge and Tran Thi Ly Bridge

From here you can see both the boats gathering and the Dragon Bridge breathing fire. Just south of the Dragon Bridge on the west bank there is also an 18–20 m tall Christmas tree – the best photo spot of the night.

3. East bank of the Han River (Tran Hung Dao street)

The east bank is less crowded than the west. Looking across, you see the whole lit-up Bach Dang waterfront, the fireworks and the fire-breathing dragon's head. It suits families with young children and anyone after a panoramic shot.

4. Bien Dong Park

Bien Dong Park also hosts a countdown. The announced plan does not mention fireworks here. The beachside atmosphere is airy with less jostling, and on the morning of 1 January there are sunrise activities to start the year.

5. Hoi An and Tam Thanh Beach Square

If you'd rather welcome the new year somewhere gentler, Hoi An has arts performances at An Hoi Sculpture Garden from 19:00 to 22:00 each evening from 30 December to 3 January. At Tam Thanh Beach Square, a show with DJs and live bands runs until 00:15 on 1 January.

Local tips for the night of 31 December

  • Eat dinner early, before 19:00. After 20:00, restaurants around the Han River are packed and food takes a long time to arrive.
  • Park a little further away and walk. Bach Dang is a pedestrian street and the surrounding roads are usually diverted on festival night. Parking on Le Loi or Tran Phu street, or on the east bank, then walking 10–15 minutes is faster than squeezing in closer.
  • Don't try to book a Grab at 00:15. Right after the fireworks, ride prices usually surge and cars struggle to get in. Sit in a café for 30–45 minutes, or walk away from the centre before booking.
  • Bring a light jacket and a raincoat. Late-December nights in Da Nang are often cool, the river breeze can be strong, and it sometimes rains.
  • With young children: write your phone number on their hand or stick it to their clothes, and agree on an easy-to-spot meeting point in advance, such as the foot of the big Christmas tree.
  • Fully charge your phone and bring a power bank. Mobile data is often slow at midnight because so many people are online.
  • Try the night run. If you enjoy staying active, sign up for the "Light Up the Han River" race to run across the bridges at 22:00, then stay on for the countdown.

What to do in Da Nang on 1 January 2027

  • Early morning: sunrise yoga at Bien Dong Park, followed by a breakfast of bun cha ca (fish cake noodle soup) or mi Quang.
  • During the day: the Marble Mountains, Son Tra Peninsula, or Ba Na Hills if the sky is clear. Buy tickets in advance on our attraction tickets page to skip the holiday queues.
  • Evening: catch Da Nang Concert 2027 at the Da Nang Museum (19:00–22:00), then wander the food space with more than 100 dishes on Bach Dang street.

Frequently asked questions

Will there be fireworks in Da Nang on New Year's Eve 2027?

Yes. According to the plan, at exactly midnight on 1 January 2027 there will be non-explosive military-grade fireworks fired from decorated boats on the Han River and along Bach Dang pedestrian street (around 300 fireworks), each show lasting about 3–5 minutes.

What time does the Da Nang 2027 countdown start?

The "Da Nang – Touching the Moment of 0h" show runs from 23:50 on 31 December to 00:15 on 1 January. The festival opening and arts performances start at 19:00 the same day.

Does the Dragon Bridge breathe fire on New Year's Eve?

Yes. The Dragon Bridge breathes fire and sprays water at exactly midnight on 1 January 2027, at the same time as the fireworks.

Do I need a ticket to watch the countdown?

The events on Bach Dang pedestrian street, along the Han River and at Bien Dong Park are in public spaces, and the announced plan does not mention any entry tickets. Only the night run requires registration.

What time should I get to the Han River?

For a good spot near the stage, arrive before 21:00. If you just want to see the fireworks from the east bank, around 22:00–22:30 is fine.

Is there a countdown in Hoi An?

Hoi An has New Year arts performances from 30 December to 3 January, but the biggest fireworks and countdown are in central Da Nang. Double-check the detailed Hoi An schedule before you go.
