The best time to stand up paddle in Da Nang is at sunrise on Man Thai or My Khe beach, during the calm-sea months (roughly April to August). City rules allow SUP only from 04:30 to 08:00 and 16:00 to 18:00, require a life jacket and ankle leash, and ban paddling in rough seas or strong wind. A board at Man Thai typically rents for about 150,000–300,000 VND (two people), and guided sunrise tours run about US$20–25 per person. These are indicative prices, so check before you go.

This guide was put together by locals. It covers the main SUP spots in Da Nang and Hoi An, the best season and hours, costs, safety rules and a few tips for a great morning on the water.

Why paddleboard in Da Nang?

  • The sun rises over the sea: the coast from Man Thai to My Khe faces east, and early-morning water is usually flat, which makes for great photos.
  • Close to town: Man Thai is about 10–15 minutes by car from the My Khe – An Thuong area.
  • Easy to learn: most beginners need only a 10–15 minute briefing before paddling seated or kneeling.
  • Clear rules and lifeguards: the city sets hours and zones, and rental operators must brief guests and send staff out with them.

4 places to SUP in Da Nang and Hoi An

1. Man Thai Beach: the classic SUP Da Nang sunrise

On summer mornings hundreds of colourful boards spread out in front of the fishing village, weaving between the fishermen's round basket boats. The beach is wide and the water is fairly calm at dawn, so it is the easiest place to start.

  • Best time: arrive 04:30–05:00 (summer sunrise is around 05:15–05:30).
  • Level: beginners; children with an adult.
  • Indicative price: about 150,000–300,000 VND per board for two. Packages with a guide and photos cost more.
  • Note: weekends and summer get very crowded, so keep your distance from other boards. Stay out of swimming areas.

2. My Khe Beach

My Khe is also recommended by many travel guides for SUP, and it suits you if you're staying at a beachfront hotel. The sunrise is just as beautiful as at Man Thai.

  • Best time: 05:00–07:00, before the sun gets strong and swimmers arrive.
  • Level: beginner to intermediate; waves are often a little bigger than at Man Thai.
  • Note: paddle only in permitted areas and check the flag at the nearest lifeguard tower.

3. Son Tra Peninsula coast

Some tours paddle along the southern shore of the peninsula, with views from the water up to the Lady Buddha at Linh Ung Pagoda and the green forest. The water is clearer, but the area is far from town and not suited to paddling alone.

  • Best time: early morning, within the 04:30–08:00 window.
  • Level: you can stand steadily and paddle for an hour.
  • Note: go only with an operator that sends a guide and safety support. Paddling into coral protection zones is banned.

4. Hoi An: Thu Bon River and Cam Thanh waterways

If ocean waves put you off, a river paddle in Hoi An is the gentle option. Tours usually start in the Cam Thanh area and paddle the Thu Bon River and the narrow channels of the nipa palm forest; some head towards the old town at sunset.

  • Best time: early morning or late afternoon.
  • Level: beginners, families with older kids.
  • Indicative price: rentals around US$10 for two hours; guided tours about US$30–40 per person.
  • Note: the coconut channels are busy with basket boats, so give way.

Safety rules

  1. Wear a life jacket and ankle leash the whole time. This is a city requirement.
  2. Don't paddle in bad weather, rough seas or strong wind. If the operator or lifeguards call a stop, accept it.
  3. Check the lifeguard flags: red flag means no entry.
  4. Stick to permitted hours and zones: no paddling into swimming areas or coral protection zones, and don't go far out.
  5. No pets, alcohol or drugs on the board.
  6. Sun protection: water-resistant sunscreen, a hat with a strap, a light long-sleeve top.
  7. Keep your phone in a dry bag around your neck or clipped to the board.

What to bring

  • Swimwear, a rash guard and dry clothes to change into
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses with a strap
  • A dry bag, towel and water
  • Small cash for rental and parking

Local tips

  • Book the evening before in summer and at weekends to get a board and guide at your preferred time.
  • Start seated or kneeling, then stand once you reach flat water. Look ahead, not at your feet.
  • For photos, shoot before 06:30 with the sun and basket boats behind you.
  • Stay close to the beach if you plan several sunrise paddles. See our stays near the beach.

Combine it with breakfast at Man Thai

A suggested morning: 04:45 arrive at Man Thai, 05:00–06:30 paddle, 06:30 watch the basket boats come in and the fish market set up on the sand, 07:00 breakfast of bun cha ca (fish cake noodle soup) at a simple local eatery near the village, 08:00 back to your hotel or on to Son Tra. Seafood is priced by the kilo, so ask before ordering.

Frequently asked questions

What hours is SUP allowed in Da Nang?

Under rules from the management board for Son Tra Peninsula and Da Nang's tourist beaches, only 04:30–08:00 and 16:00–18:00. For sunrise, arrive around 04:30–05:00.

Can I paddleboard if I can't swim?

Yes, if you wear a life jacket and leash, stay close to shore and go with staff. Choose a very calm day, or paddle the river in Hoi An.

How much does stand up paddle in Da Nang cost?

As a guide, a board at Man Thai is about 150,000–300,000 VND for two people, and guided tours about US$20–25 per person. Weekend and peak-season prices can be higher, so check first.

Can I SUP in Da Nang from October to December?

Only on some days. The rainy season and northeast monsoon bring big waves and many days are closed. If you get a calm morning you can go; otherwise the river in Hoi An is safer.

Can kids join?

Children can sit on a board with an adult, wearing a life jacket that fits. Minimum ages vary by operator, so ask in advance.
