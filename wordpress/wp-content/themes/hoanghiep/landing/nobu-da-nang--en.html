Overview

Where Japanese minimalism meets the sea

Developed by VCRE (Viet Capital Real Estate) under licence from Nobu Hospitality – the lifestyle brand founded by Robert De Niro, Chef Nobu Matsuhisa and Meir Teper – Nobu Residences Da Nang brings the Nobu way of living to Vietnam's most celebrated beach city.

The tower's silhouette draws on the Japanese folding fan, or sensu, and the garden principle of shakkei – "borrowed scenery" – framing uninterrupted views of the East Sea, the Son Tra Peninsula and the Han River. Residences are finished in a calm Japandi palette and supported by hotel-grade services delivered by Nobu Hospitality.

Ownership

Buying as an international purchaser

Published sources differ on the tenure of Nobu Residences Da Nang. Sales materials describe long-term ownership for Vietnamese buyers and a 50-year term for foreign buyers, while other sources refer to a land-use term to 2060 on commercial–service land. Before any reservation, we review the draft Sale and Purchase Agreement with you and confirm, in writing:

  • your eligibility to purchase as a foreign individual or company;
  • the ownership term that applies to your residence;
  • the payment schedule, handover standard and service charges;
  • the terms of any rental programme operated by Nobu Hospitality.

A rental programme managed by Nobu Hospitality is offered to owners. Some sales materials cite an indicative return of around 6% per year; the actual terms depend on the programme agreement and should be read carefully before you commit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is Nobu Residences Da Nang?

At 01 Vo Van Kiet Street, on the corner of Vo Nguyen Giap, An Hai Ward, Da Nang – directly on My Khe Beach.

Who is the developer?

VCRE (Viet Capital Real Estate), through the project company Circle Point Real Estate JSC, under licence from Nobu Hospitality. The main contractor is Newtecons.

What residence types are available?

Studios, one-, two- and three-bedroom residences including Dual Key layouts, six Sky Villas with private pools and two penthouses – from approximately 42 m² to 372 m².

How much does a residence cost?

One-bedroom residences are indicatively priced from approximately VND 6.6 to 9.1 billion. Prices for other types are available on request and vary by floor, view and layout.

Can foreigners buy at Nobu Residences Da Nang?

Foreign buyers' eligibility and ownership term should be confirmed against the Sale and Purchase Agreement. We review the documents with you before any reservation.

When will the project be completed?

Construction began in Q2 2024. Completion and handover are expected in 2027; by October 2026 the structure had reached level 22.
